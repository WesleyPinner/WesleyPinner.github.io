Composting Game

This is a basic game where the objective is to compost. You can grow food, cook dishes, and compost the resulting waste in order to grow more food more quickly. You make money both selling items directly and fulfilling orders. Different tabs in the game contain different parts, such as "crafting" for cooking dishes and "orders" for orders.

For more information about composting, you can visit websites such as Compost Collective for Auckland. Compost collective offers coupons for composting bins to anyone who participates in a free composting course, so if you are in Auckland and are interested in composting this is a great opportunity for you!
